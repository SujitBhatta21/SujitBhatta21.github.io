A group machine learning coursework (IN3063) with two tasks: CIFAR-10 image classification and histopathological cancer detection. Built with Ivan Radavskyi, Wael Khafagi, Yaseen Mneimneih, and MD Iftier Roshid.

**My contributions:** Task 1 neural network architecture and training pipeline; Task 2 CNN model evaluation.

---

## Tasks

**Task 1 - CIFAR-10 Classification**
- Dataset: 60,000 32×32 colour images across 10 classes
- Built and trained a CNN from scratch with Conda environment management

**Task 2 - Lung & Colon Cancer Detection**
- Dataset: 25,000 histopathological images across 5 classes (lung: aca, n, scc; colon: aca, n)
- CNN model trained on CUDA 13.0 with PyTorch via Anaconda Navigator
